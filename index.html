Nakuru County Pilot — March to May 2026

Running our V1 pilot across Olenguruone and Kiptagich. Target: 50 registered farmers, 5 active buyers, and 20 completed M-Pesa transactions by May 31, 2026.

Our mission

Every farmer in Kenya should be able to list their harvest and receive fair payment the same day — regardless of whether they have a smartphone, an internet connection, or even a phone at all. MazaoLink builds the infrastructure that makes this possible: USSD, SMS, WhatsApp, a mobile app, and village agents, all feeding into one smart backend. No middlemen. No delays. No unfair prices.

The team

Who's building this

A solo developer and a network of trusted village agents — lean, fast, and deeply rooted in the community we serve.

SL

Solo Developer

Full-stack build — backend API, USSD flows, SMS webhooks, WhatsApp bot, React Native app, and buyer portal. Four channels, one system, one builder.

VA

Village Agents

3–4 trusted community members from Olenguruone and Kiptagich who onboard non-smartphone farmers, manage listings, and act as local support.

FK

Founding Farmers

The 15–20 farmers interviewed in Week 1. Their real pain points became our product specification. They are MazaoLink's co-creators.

Tech stack

  • ⚙️ Backend — Node.js + Express + Supabase
  • 📟 USSD & SMS — Africa's Talking API
  • 💬 WhatsApp — Twilio WhatsApp API
  • 💳 Payments — M-Pesa Daraja API
  • 📱 Farmer App — React Native (Expo)
  • 🌐 Buyer Portal — Next.js
  • ☁️ Hosting — Railway
